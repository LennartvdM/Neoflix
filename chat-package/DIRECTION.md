# The direction, in the owner's words (2026-11, in order)

These are Lennart's messages from the Claude Code session that built the
current design, quoted as he wrote them. Where he sent an image, the
note in brackets says what it showed. Later messages refine earlier ones.

1. "Alright I do think we reverted to a typical generic claude design (not to drag your design it's great for most purposes but do consider that because you're now so popular your design appears on half the modern websites today) It's the typography, the box outlining and everything, and I fear it put us on the wrong track. We're building self-assembling interface layouts. I'm thinking about bento but far more dynamic that a rigid grid 'bento' is more a reference to the size balance between the shapes."
2. "Again, the bento was only a reference for shape size and orientation balance. In a modern site these are all their own elements that magnetically clamp to each other depending on the context, that's a self-asembling interface"
3. "In my repo vragenlijst-executive search I have a scrollytell"
4. "I think the sidebar is a good start, it's the user's anchor. But what I think could be more interesting given the Neoflix material is that the sidebar could expand, branch, morph, and rather than a simple vertical scrolly tell, the site has a horizontal dimension as well. But also pagenation to breakk up the prezi feel"
5. "That's what I mean, we need to get the ontology right to always give the user the information they need, the information they could want (without competing for what they need) and without overwhelming it with showing the entire index. Your typography is terrible here, better to just adopt my executive search language for now"
6. "Yeah this was a bit of a petty side-track, but a necessary one. Alright back to graphs"
7. "Let's make the graph edges broad dashed lines white, or at least low contrast. So it's more like abstract whiteboard and less circuitboard"
8. [An image of content with related items around it] "I'm thinking about the principle of 'percolating content'. Every item has adjacent / related content percolating from it"
9. "The neighbours tether to content outside the screen, it's out going, internal tether would be a mess"
10. [Images of earlier Neoflix layouts] "Do you remember these layouts? I liked these. Especially this one. I think clearly demarcated videos and feathered videos could be part of the design language, menaing different things"
11. [An image of the resulting page] "Beautiful. That's the direction."
12. "Maybe overlaps as a language as well. under and over and separate meaning different things"
13. "Try to avoid warm colours. warm is a deviation from sterility. But yeah I guess we now need to think more about structuring the content, possibly rewriting content, finding new categories, and creating space for abstract explanatory animations/moving illustrations, but first as placeholders not to commit too many tokens to it yet, pure ontology"
14. "I feel the animations need to hit the edges, like an intermission so it doesn't feel all cluttered"
15. "That's fine, let's inventorise all our material thus far before we start finding ontology in it"
16. "I think it'd be cool to turn her papers, the open licensed at least into stories. BUT without changing the worder, at best side notations or intermissions or a guidance layer just the material stays the same as me rewriting her phd work would be inappropriate (even for a prototype, I first want to show what it can do and afterwards she'll get to join the Claude session and give it her directions, it's just that as for now she has no idea what's possible she needs to hold something first before she can refine it) And even the two that aren't licensed can be turned into synopsis or cliff notes or whatever. And I feel that this is also something that will provide us with chunks of content to (intelligently) scatter and pad and guide other content with. Same for the video, we can turn it into a transcript, cut it up (this is my production so I can do whatever I want with it) and use it as morsels elsewhere."
17. [He supplied the transcript of the session film, then a context package for it: timed subtitles, a storyboard and keyframes.]
18. "Let's put this into a package that I can bring to a Claude chat (not code) session. It must include the code though, so it can build its own artefacts." (This package.)
